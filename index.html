About Me

Hi, I’m Audrey, a photographer currently finishing my senior year at the University of Connecticut, where I’m earning a dual degree in Fine Arts and Ecology & Evolutionary Biology. While I specialize in wildlife and nature photography, I also enjoy capturing a wide range of subjects including events, portraits, and sporting events, bringing the same attention and care to each project I work on.

In my personal work, I often experiment with older film and digital cameras, allowing each piece of equipment to bring its own unique character to the images I create. Through my photography, I aim to inspire curiosity, connection, and a deeper appreciation for both people and the world around us.
